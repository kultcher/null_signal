Looking for feedback on my cyberpunk "guy in the chair" hacking game (~15 min)

Been working on a game called Null Signal. It's a terminal-based hacking game where you're on overwatch for an on-the-ground operative. Inspired by Shadowrun and Cyberpunk, but set in a post-singularity world. You have to think fast under pressure as you hack cameras and drones, distract enemies, decrypt locks and deal with hostile IC while.
There's a lot going on in the game, so what I'm sharing is basically an intro/tutorial level to give a sense of the world and gameplay.
Any feedback welcome but honestly, at this point I'm mostly looking for a "Does this seem like fun?" vibe-check.
Also, happy to trade playtest time/feedback with others, just shoot me DM.
